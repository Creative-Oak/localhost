**This is the clause that matters. Please read it.**

Localhost can send termination signals to processes running under your user
account. A terminated process stops immediately and **any unsaved work in it is
lost.** That is the intended behaviour of the feature, not a defect.

Localhost identifies a process by its process ID together with its start time,
and re-checks both immediately before signalling, specifically so that a stale
listing cannot cause the wrong process to be signalled. This reduces that risk;
it does not eliminate every possible failure.

work, interrupted services, or any consequence of a process being stopped,
